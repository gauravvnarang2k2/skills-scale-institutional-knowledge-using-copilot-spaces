# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme follows a structured lifecycle that moves from initiation to planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. During initiation, teams validate business need, define success metrics, align stakeholders, and capture early risks before approving work to enter planning. Planning then turns approved initiatives into a prioritized and estimated backlog with clear acceptance criteria, milestones, dependencies, and a documented Definition of Done.

Execution focuses on transparent delivery and consistent team rhythm. Work is tracked on a project board through clear status stages, and teams maintain cadence through standups, weekly syncs, and milestone demos. Pull requests are expected to include issue and acceptance context, stay manageable in size when possible, and complete CI checks before merge review.

Roles and communication responsibilities are explicit to reduce ambiguity and improve coordination. Project Managers own delivery orchestration, risk tracking, and cross-team dependency management; Product Managers drive outcomes and prioritization; Developers and QA collaborate to implement and validate against acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide direction and approvals. Communication is grounded in regular status updates, risk visibility, and defined escalation paths for blockers and incidents.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout delivery and release. OctoAcme combines unit, integration, and critical-flow smoke tests with linting and security scanning in CI, plus manual QA when needed for feature acceptance. Release guidance adds pre-release readiness checks, deployment and verification checklists, and rollback preparedness, while retrospectives convert lessons learned into actionable improvements tracked in the backlog.
